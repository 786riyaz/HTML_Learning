# iFrame Notes

---

## `<iframe>` (Inline Frame)

### Purpose
- Allows you to **embed another webpage or external content** within a webpage  

---

## Key Attributes

### `src`
- Specifies the **URL of the content** to be embedded  

### `width` and `height`
- Used to define the **dimensions** of the iframe  

---

## Example

```html
<iframe 
  src="https://example.com" 
  width="600" 
  height="400">
</iframe>
